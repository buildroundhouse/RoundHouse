# RoundHouse — History

## Creation

History is a private Entity tied to the person whose activity created the history inside it.

RoundHouse creates one History Entity after the person first completes a legitimate Property or Business connection. A new account remains in intake until that first connection is complete.

## Access and presentation

Only the person tied to History can access it. Their Role there is **History Viewer**, using Viewer as the only Role within the History Entity. Access is available with a paid or unpaid subscription.

History appears alongside the person's other contexts in the Profile switcher. Its Entity name is **History**. Selecting it opens the same familiar Timeline structure with its limited functions.

History remains available while the person has current Property or Business relationships. If all those relationships end, sign-in opens the remaining History context rather than requiring the person to repeat first-time intake.

## Combined Timeline

History presents the person's legitimate activity across their past and present Properties and Businesses in one chronological stream. This includes their Notes, messages, comments, CAPTURE, Work, Jobs, photos, Resolutions, milestones, accomplishments, ratings, and other attributed activity.

Each item keeps its original Property or Business, Role, authority, Job or Work context, date, and authorship. History displays the underlying record without moving it out of its source or duplicating it.

A change in Role, authority, employment, ownership, or access preserves the person's previous contributions. Current access to a former Property or Business ends when its governing permission ends. Historical visibility is limited to what the person is entitled to retain and does not expose new activity or other participants' private records.

## Functions

The person can view and search the combined Timeline.

History's active functions are **Share RoundHouse** and the paths to search for, connect to, or begin setup of a Property or Business.

Notes, CAPTURE, messages, comments, Work, appointments, and participant management are created within the relevant Property or Business, using the Role and authority established there. They are not created in History.

History Viewer does not earn points, badges, or status progression. Historical accomplishments remain part of the Timeline.

## Connection and setup

Search and setup use the intake screens in **`architecture/screens/01_ROUNDHOUSE_INTAKE_SCREENS.md`**, preserving completed personal Identity information.

Creation limits, the paid-party rule, requested ownership transfer, and Admin eligibility follow **`architecture/ROUNDHOUSE_ROLES_AND_PERMISSIONS.md`**. Starting from History does not grant authority in the destination Property or Business.

## Acceptance checks

- A brand-new account with no completed Property or Business connection remains in intake and has no History Entity.
- The first completed connection creates exactly one private History Entity; later connections use that same History.
- Only the associated person can enter History, regardless of any other person's ownership or management authority.
- Contributions from past and present contexts appear with their original attribution.
- Role changes and loss of the last Property or Business connection preserve History without restoring former workspace access.
- Share RoundHouse and connection/setup remain available; content creation and management occur only in the authorized destination context.
