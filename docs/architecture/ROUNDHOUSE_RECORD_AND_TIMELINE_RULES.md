# ROUNDHOUSE — RECORD & TIMELINE RULES

## Purpose

Defines how Roundhouse records are preserved and presented.

## 1. Records

A Record documents activity connected to a Property, Business, or participant.

A Record may include work performed, photos, documents, receipts, approvals, communications, and other documented activity.

The same Record may appear in the appropriate Property, Business, and participant histories without being duplicated.

## 2. Timeline

The Timeline presents the chronological history of a Property or Business.

Records remain connected to the people and Entities that legitimately participated in them.

## 3. History

History preserves the underlying Record even when it is no longer shown normally on a Timeline or portfolio.

Changing ownership, employment, roles, or access does not erase legitimate history.

## 4. Record Status

A Record may be:

**Accepted · Not Accepted · Rejected · Disputed**

Status affects how the Record is presented. It does not erase the underlying Record.

## 5. Corrections & Disputes

A Record may be corrected, disputed, or disassociated from an Entity when necessary.

Disassociation removes an incorrect Entity connection without erasing the contributor's legitimate underlying history.

## 6. Visibility

Public and private settings determine who can see a Record.

Visibility does not determine whether the underlying Record exists.

## Governing Rule

**Roundhouse preserves what happened, who participated, and where it legitimately occurred. The Timeline is the presentation of that history.**
