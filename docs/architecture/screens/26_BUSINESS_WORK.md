# Roundhouse Business Work

## Purpose

**Business Work** is the top right-edge working screen inside the Business Entity.

Its home is:

**Business Entity → Work**

It answers:

**What does this Business have on its plate across all Properties?**

Business Work organizes collective responsibility and execution across the Business without becoming a heavy project-management dashboard.

The visual direction uses a **lean tiered Work Flow Rail** inspired by a timeline / bracket structure rather than a full Kanban board or literal tournament bracket.

**Business Work organizes responsibility and execution across the Business. Property Work narrows that same reality to one place. CAPTURE proves what actually happened during the work session.**

---

# 1. Position Inside the Business Entity

The Business Entity right-edge order remains:

1. **Work**
2. **Team**
3. **Properties**
4. **Vault**

Work occupies the same top position used for Property Work and personal Daily Grind, preserving useful muscle memory while changing scope.

**Daily Grind = what I need to do.**  
**Property Work = what needs doing at this Property.**  
**Business Work = what the Business has on its plate across all Properties.**

---

# 2. Business Work Screen

Work opens as a Business-scoped working sheet over the Business Timeline.

At the top:

**Work**  
Business name beneath it  
**Search Work**                 **+ Create Work**

The Create Work control appears only when the current person's authority permits creating Business work.

Business Work should feel operational and composed, not like a large enterprise project-management product.

---

# 3. The Work Flow Rail

The default visual structure is a **tiered Work Flow Rail**.

It borrows the clarity of a timeline and the branching logic of a wrestling / tournament bracket, but stays much leaner.

The governing lifecycle is:

**Requested → Open → Assigned → In Progress → Complete → Verified**

with **Cancelled** as a terminal exception.

Instead of six large Kanban columns, the screen uses a slim visual rail showing those lifecycle stages in order.

Each stage is a tier on the rail. Work cards belonging to that stage sit beside it and visually connect to that tier with a short branch line.

Conceptually:

**Requested**  ── work cards  
↓  
**Open**       ── work cards  
↓  
**Assigned**   ── work cards  
↓  
**In Progress** ── work cards  
↓  
**Complete**   ── work cards  
↓  
**Verified**   ── recent verified work when useful

The rail communicates movement without requiring the user to drag cards between giant columns.

The branch / tier visual should be subtle and refined, not decorative or game-like.

---

# 4. Why the Rail Is Better Than a Full Bracket

A literal tournament bracket would become visually chaotic as the Business accumulates many jobs.

The Flow Rail preserves the useful idea:

**work enters → gets accepted → gets assigned → gets done → gets verified**

while allowing many Work items to sit cleanly beside each stage.

The result should feel like a **business workflow timeline with bracket DNA**, not a wrestling graphic.

---

# 5. Needs Attention

**Needs Attention** is not a lifecycle stage.

It is a priority condition that can apply to Work in any stage.

Examples:

- overdue;
- blocked by a Resolution;
- failed / returned verification;
- missing critical information;
- urgent homeowner request;
- unassigned work that has aged too long;
- scheduled work approaching without required preparation.

Needs Attention should appear as a compact priority strip above the Flow Rail.

Tapping it filters the existing Work cards rather than moving them into a duplicate status bucket.

---

# 6. Work Cards

Business Work cards stay compact.

The Business-level card adds **Property context** as a first-class element.

A card may show:

- Work title;
- Property name / address shorthand;
- assignee;
- due date;
- priority when meaningful;
- current lifecycle state;
- related Asset when useful;
- scheduled time indicator when useful;
- attention / blocker indicator when needed.

Example:

**Replace Rear Exterior Light**  
110 Dripping Springs  
Assigned: Mike  
Due Sept. 18  
**Assigned**

Example:

**Annual HVAC Service**  
Spring Lake Residence  
Assigned: Carlos  
Tomorrow · 9:00 AM  
**Assigned**

Example:

**Investigate Pool Pump Noise**  
Lake Travis House  
Unassigned · High Priority  
**Open**

The card should not display every relationship or piece of metadata at once.

---

# 7. Stage Expansion

Each Flow Rail tier can be expanded or collapsed.

This lets an Owner / Manager focus on one part of the pipeline without losing the overall lifecycle context.

Example:

**Assigned · 8**  
shows the eight compact Work cards currently assigned.

Collapsed tiers retain their count.

The rail itself remains visible so the screen still communicates where work sits in the lifecycle.

---

# 8. Filtering Without Breaking the Rail

Business Work may be filtered by:

- Property;
- assignee / team member;
- due date;
- priority;
- work category;
- related Asset;
- attention state.

Filtering changes which cards appear beside the existing lifecycle rail.

It should not redraw the interface into a different dashboard every time a filter is selected.

Useful quick filters can live directly under Search Work.

---

# 9. Search

**Search Work** searches Work available to the current person across the Business.

Search can match useful terms such as:

- Work title;
- Property;
- client / homeowner where permitted;
- assignee;
- category;
- Asset;
- external / PO reference;
- useful description terms.

Search preserves the lifecycle structure by showing matching cards at their actual stage.

---

# 10. Create Work

**+ Create Work** opens a lightweight creation flow.

Because Business Work spans many Properties, Property is an important early field.

The creation flow may include:

- Property;
- title;
- description;
- category;
- priority;
- requester / creator;
- assignee when known;
- due date when useful;
- related Asset;
- optional photos / files;
- optional PO / external reference.

The user should not need to complete every field before creating a legitimate Work item.

---

# 11. Work Detail

Tapping a Business Work card opens **Work Detail** for the same underlying Work Record used in Property Work.

The Business context may emphasize:

- Work title;
- Property;
- current lifecycle state;
- requester / creator;
- assignee;
- due date;
- priority;
- related Asset;
- Calendar relationship;
- linked Resolution;
- CAPTURE evidence;
- Work comments;
- photos / files;
- Estimate / Invoice relationship;
- chronological activity;
- verification state.

The goal is one coherent Work Record, not a Business copy and a Property copy.

---

# 12. Property Relationship

Every Property-based Business Work item clearly identifies its Property.

Tapping the Property identity from Work Detail can provide the established Property action path, including **Enter Property** when permitted.

Entering the Property shows the same Work item in:

**Property Entity → Work**

No duplicate Work item is created.

---

# 13. Assignment

Authorized Owners / Managers can assign or reassign Work to appropriate Trade Team Members or approved outside trade partners.

Assignment moves the same Work Record into the **Assigned** stage of the Flow Rail.

The assigned person's own working views may surface the assignment according to permission.

Assignment does not expose unrelated Business or Property information.

---

# 14. Start Work and CAPTURE

For an authorized assigned worker, Work Detail can expose:

**Start Work**

This moves into the established CAPTURE workflow with known context carried forward:

- Business / acting identity;
- Property;
- Work item;
- related Asset when one exists.

When actual execution begins, the Work item moves to **In Progress** according to governing workflow logic.

CAPTURE remains responsible for documenting the real work session.

---

# 15. Calendar

Business Work shows scheduling context where useful but does not become a second Calendar.

A Work card may show:

- scheduled date / time;
- upcoming indicator;
- confirmation state when materially useful.

Calendar remains the governing scheduling and dispatch system.

---

# 16. Resolution

If Work is blocked by a genuine unresolved dependency, the Work item may link to a Resolution.

The Work card can show a compact blocker indicator while remaining in its actual lifecycle stage.

Example:

**Assigned**  
Blocked: homeowner approval

The item does not move into a fake "blocked" lifecycle stage merely because a Resolution exists.

---

# 17. Comments and Mail

Work comments remain attached to the Work Record because they concern execution of that job.

Roundhouse Mail remains the communication system between people.

**Work comments = job discussion.**  
**Mail = person-to-person / Entity communication.**

---

# 18. Completion and Verification

When the worker indicates execution is finished, Work moves to **Complete**.

Where verification is required, an authorized Owner / Manager can review the result and move it to **Verified**.

Complete and Verified remain distinct because "worker finished" and "authorized party reviewed" are not always the same event.

Verified Work should not remain visually dominant forever. Recent verified items may remain available near the bottom of the rail, while older completed history remains discoverable through the appropriate Property / Business historical Record views.

---

# 19. Mobile / Narrow Layout

On narrow screens, the Business Work Flow Rail should not force a cramped multi-column layout.

The same lifecycle becomes a slim vertical rail with expandable stages and stacked cards.

The rail remains readable with one-handed scrolling.

Roundhouse should not shrink six horizontal columns until the content becomes unusable.

---

# 20. Larger-Screen Layout

On wider screens, the Flow Rail may gain more breathing room and subtle horizontal branching from each lifecycle tier.

The screen should still avoid a dense enterprise Kanban board.

Property, assignee, due date, and status remain the main scan points.

The visual hierarchy should feel intentionally designed rather than data-heavy.

---

# 21. Relationship to Property Work

Property Work remains leaner because its Property context is already known.

Business Work adds only the information necessary for cross-Property management:

- Property identity;
- broader assignment visibility;
- cross-Property filtering;
- Business-wide attention state.

The underlying Work item and lifecycle stay the same.

---

# 22. Permissions

Business Work is permission-aware.

Examples:

- Owners / Managers may see Business-wide Work according to authority;
- Trade Team Members see only Work legitimately available to them;
- outside trade partners see Work deliberately shared / assigned to them;
- Property participants do not gain Business-wide visibility merely because one Work item concerns their Property;
- Viewers remain view-only where permitted.

The Flow Rail only displays Work the current person is authorized to see.

---

# 23. Visual Direction

Business Work should feel like a **clean operating pipeline**, not a spreadsheet and not a project-management board.

Key visual rules:

- Business Timeline remains underneath the working sheet;
- lifecycle is expressed through one slim tiered Flow Rail;
- Work cards branch from the stage they currently occupy;
- Property is prominent on every cross-Property card;
- Needs Attention floats above lifecycle rather than becoming another stage;
- cards stay compact;
- stages can collapse;
- no giant colored columns;
- no decorative tournament graphics;
- no duplicate Work records between Business and Property views.

The bracket inspiration should be felt in the structure, not seen as a gimmick.

---

## Governing Relationship

**Business Work = what the Business has on its plate across Properties.**  
**Property Work = what needs doing at one place.**  
**Daily Grind = what one person needs to do.**  
**CAPTURE = what actually happened during execution.**

---

## Governing Rule

**Business Work uses a lean tiered Work Flow Rail to show how the Business's Work moves from request through verification across Properties. The rail provides bracket-like progression without the visual weight of a full Kanban board or tournament bracket, while every Work item remains one shared Record across Business and Property views.**
