# ROUNDHOUSE — LISTS, NOTES, REMINDERS & RECEIPTS

## Purpose

Lists, Notes, Reminders, and Receipts are **Command Center working functions**, not separate destination screens.

They live in the right-side working rail with Daily Grind and open the same way: as a large working sheet over the Command Center rather than navigating the person away from it.

The governing distinction is important:

**Something can relate to a Property without automatically becoming part of the homeowner-visible Property record.**

Trade Professionals and Businesses need private working information to operate a job. Information becomes shared with the Property only through a deliberate sharing action.

## Right-Side Working Rail

The right-side Command Center rail is arranged vertically:

1. **Daily Grind**
2. **Tasks / Lists**
3. **Receipts**
4. **Properties**

These tabs remain visible when one of the working sheets is open. Tapping another tab swaps the working sheet without leaving the Command Center.

## How the Working Sheets Open

Tasks / Lists and Receipts use the same physical behavior established by Daily Grind.

The selected function opens like a **large sheet over the Command Center**, covering roughly 90% of the screen. It covers the top Profile/Entity/Rewards/Mail area and the Timeline/Daily Log, reaching almost completely to the left edge while leaving a clipped visual hint of the underlying Search area.

The **right-side tabs remain exposed and active**. The **bottom Command Center buttons remain exposed**.

The center **CAPTURE/Concierge button continues to dip upward into the open sheet**, just as it dips into the Timeline and Daily Grind. If Concierge is already active, the active/animated CAPTURE state remains available while working in these functions.

This makes the interaction feel like opening a working pad over the Command Center rather than opening another screen.

# Tasks / Lists

Opening Tasks / Lists does **not** display the contents of every list at once.

It opens a clean master index modeled on the simplicity of Apple Reminders. A **+** button appears at the upper-right for creating a new list.

The person's own named lists appear first. Examples may include:

- Shopping
- Materials
- Tomorrow
- Punch List
- Personal Reminders

Below the person's lists, Roundhouse shows the **Properties/jobs the person is currently working on or still has active work associated with**.

Tapping a Property expands it to reveal the lists associated with that Property. Collapsing it returns the person to the master list view.

A Property does not need to clutter the master list before it is relevant. Once the person begins creating lists or working information associated with that Property, it can appear in the Property portion of the index.

## Private and Shared Lists

A list associated with a Property is not automatically visible to the homeowner or every participant.

A Property-related list may be:

**Private / Internal** — working information belonging to the person or Business. It can be associated with the Property without entering the homeowner-visible Property record.

**Shared** — deliberately shared with specific authorized Property participants.

Being associated with the same Property does not automatically grant another Trade Professional, worker, homeowner, or participant access to the list.

# Notes & Reminders

Notes and reminders can operate within the person's working lists and Property/job context, but their audience must always be unmistakable.

A company may create an **internal note about a Property**. The note still happened in the context of that Property and can be retrieved by the Business through that job, but it remains a Business working record rather than becoming part of the homeowner-visible Property record.

Example:

**INTERNAL COMPANY NOTE — 110 Dripping Springs**  
There was a disagreement with the homeowner about cabinet placement. Review the approved drawing before discussing future changes.

An internal company note can be visible to the worker and appropriately authorized Business Owner/Manager without being visible to the homeowner.

A note deliberately contributed to the shared Property record must look materially different:

**PROPERTY NOTE — SHARED WITH PROPERTY**

Roundhouse should not rely on a tiny privacy icon or subtle toggle for this distinction. Internal and Property-shared notes should be visually and verbally different during creation.

An internal note must **never become homeowner-visible through an accidental single tap**. Moving or sharing information from internal Business records into the Property record requires a deliberate action and confirmation.

The same principle applies to reminders. A reminder may concern work at a Property without becoming a shared Property Record.

# Receipts

Receipts is a fast working receipt bin organized around active/open jobs.

When the Receipts tab opens, the person sees the names of the Properties/jobs they are currently working on or still have open. The bottom **CAPTURE** control remains visible.

The person selects the appropriate job and uses CAPTURE to photograph the receipt. The receipt is saved to the Trade Professional's or Business's receipt records for that job.

## Default Receipt Rule

Saving a receipt under a Property/job does **not** automatically add that receipt to the homeowner-visible Property record.

For example, saving a receipt under **110 Dripping Springs** means the receipt is associated with the Trade Professional's work at 110 Dripping Springs. It remains an internal working receipt unless deliberately transferred/shared through the customer-purchase process.

This protects internal purchasing information, supplier choices, material costs, margins, and other Business information that the customer may not be entitled to inspect merely because the purchase supported their job.

## Customer Purchase

When materials are being purchased directly for the Property owner—for example, using the customer's credit card or passing the purchase directly through to the customer—the person can deliberately choose **Customer Purchase**.

Roundhouse then presents an explicit confirmation explaining that the receipt will become part of the customer's Property record and that the purchase is being treated as a direct customer purchase rather than an internal marked-up Business purchase.

Only after confirmation is the receipt added directly to the Property record for the homeowner.

The homeowner then has the Property copy/record of that receipt. The Trade Professional still retains the historical attribution showing that they captured or submitted it.

# Governing Rules

1. **Daily Grind, Tasks / Lists, Receipts, and Properties are neighboring right-side Command Center working functions.**
2. Their working sheets open over the Command Center rather than navigating to a separate destination screen.
3. The right-side rail and bottom Command Center controls remain visible while these working sheets are open.
4. CAPTURE/Concierge continues to protrude into and operate with the open working sheet.
5. **Property context does not equal Property ownership or homeowner visibility of a Record.**
6. Trade/Business Lists, Notes, Reminders, and Receipts are private/internal by default unless deliberately shared.
7. Internal Business information can remain associated with the Property/job so the Business can retrieve it in context without placing it in the homeowner-visible Property record.
8. Shared Property information must be visually and functionally distinct from internal Business information.
9. Internal Notes must not become Property-shared through an accidental single action.
10. Receipts remain internal job receipts by default. Customer Purchase is a deliberate exception that can place a receipt into the homeowner's Property record.
11. Daily Grind may surface a relevant Note, Reminder, List item, or other working item when it matters to today's plan, but Daily Grind does not create a duplicate copy or become the owner of that information.
