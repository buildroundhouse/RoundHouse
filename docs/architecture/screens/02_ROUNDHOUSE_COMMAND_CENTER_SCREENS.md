# Roundhouse Command Center Screens

## Purpose

The Command Center is the personal working screen for a Roundhouse Avatar.

It is not the Property or Business Entity page. The Command Center is where the person works. Entity pages are where the shared history of a Property or Business is viewed and, according to Role and permission, worked within.

**Avatar Timeline = what I am doing.**  
**Entity Timeline = what is happening here.**

The Timeline is the architectural and visual center of the Command Center.

---

# 1. Trade Professional Command Center

Upon completing Intake or signing in, the Trade Professional lands on their personal Command Center, operating through their currently selected Business Entity, such as DMT DESIGN BUILD.

The main Timeline shows the Trade Professional's own attributed activity: work, photographs, lists, client interactions, accomplishments, Resolutions, and other participation. Coworker activity does not automatically stream into the personal Timeline.

## Top Bar — Four Areas

The top bar is shallow and contains four controls/areas from left to right.

### 1. Avatar Photo — far left

Tap the Avatar photo to open the Avatar/Role menu.

From here the person can switch between Businesses, Properties, or other Entity Roles they operate through and access appropriate Role/profile information and settings.

A person operating through more than one Business can switch the active Business here.

### 2. Current Business Logo + Name — beside Avatar

The current Business logo and Business name form one tappable area.

Tap either the logo or name to leave the personal Command Center and open the Business Entity page.

The Business Entity page is a separate screen. It is intentionally more limited than the Command Center and centers on the Business's collective Timeline and appropriate Business-level information. A large representative company photograph visually distinguishes the Entity page from the person's Command Center.

A clear Back arrow returns the person to their personal Command Center and working tools.

### 3. Badge + Points — toward the right

Shows the Avatar's current Roundhouse status and points.

Tap to open Rewards, progress, available rewards, and points history.

### 4. Inbox — far right

One combined Inbox for messages and notifications.

There is no separate permanent message button and notification bell.

---

## Timeline

The Timeline dominates the Command Center.

It is a literal visual timeline, not a social feed: a vertical time spine with compact event bubbles/cards positioned along it. Events alternate side-to-side and connect to the spine at their relevant times. Several events should normally be visible at once, with spacing able to suggest elapsed time.

The Timeline opens to **Today**.

Today begins expanded. The person can collapse it into a broader multi-day view. Collapsed days retain the same visual timeline metaphor and show a representative photograph, date, and small summary/count.

The hierarchy is:

**Multiple Days → Day → Event / Job / Task → Photos & Details**

Tap a day to expand that day's activity. Tap an event to open its photographs and details.

### Timeline Search

A small magnifying-glass icon sits in the **upper-left corner of the Timeline**.

Tap it and a search field expands across/over the Timeline. On the personal Trade Professional Timeline the placeholder is:

**Search your Timeline…**

Search filters the Timeline itself. It does not open a conventional results page. Nonmatching events temporarily disappear while the matching chronological visual history remains.

Examples include `drywall`, `Johnson`, `electrical`, or `cabinet install`.

A Trade Professional searching their personal Timeline searches their own attributed history across Entities. If they no longer have permission to enter a particular Property Entity, they may still see their own historical participation there, but Search does not grant access to other people's activity or to the underlying Entity.

Clear or close Search to restore the full Timeline.

**Search filters the current Timeline. Avatar Timeline search filters that Avatar's history. Entity Timeline search filters the Entity's history according to viewer permissions.**

---

## Right Edge — Four Timeline Tabs

Four small icon/handle tabs are attached to the right edge of the Timeline. They remain visually quiet at rest and open over the Timeline rather than replacing its importance.

### 1. Daily Grind

Opens today's working plan: scheduled work, assignments, carryovers, personal items, reminders, attention items, and today's Resolutions.

Daily Grind automatically opens as a near-full-screen overlay over the Timeline on login and can be dismissed back to its right-edge tab.

Daily Grind belongs to the person. For Owners or Managers it may also surface the Daily Grinds of people they manage according to authority and privacy rules. Private calendar details remain private; management may see only that the person is blocked or unavailable where appropriate.

### 2. Tasks / Lists

Opens the person's working tasks, checklists, and lists.

### 3. Receipts

Opens receipts and related material documentation available to the person.

### 4. Properties

Opens Properties the person can legitimately access and provides the direct doorway into a client Property Entity.

---

## Bottom Bar — Five Controls

Five primarily icon-based controls with short labels sit very low across the bottom of the screen.

### 1. Resolution Toggle — far left

Uses a toggle/switch icon.

Tap to open Resolutions — everything the person still has "on the hook."

The full Resolution system is governed separately and is not defined on this screen.

### 2. People — left-center

Opens people legitimately known through Roundhouse participation.

For an Owner or Manager, this is also the deliberate path to a Team Member's personal work history:

**People → Team Member → Team Member Timeline**

Subordinate activity is not dumped into the Owner's or Manager's personal Timeline.

### 3. CAPTURE — center

The dominant center control. It projects/dips upward into the Timeline more than the other bottom controls.

Tap CAPTURE to open the camera immediately.

After capture, Roundhouse records the photograph with its time and appropriate context and allows an optional notation or Fill In Later path. A Notation Only path is also available when a photograph is unnecessary.

There is one primary camera entry point. A second permanent upper camera button is not required.

### 4. Estimates / Invoices — right-center

Opens the estimating and invoicing workspace.

### 5. Calendar — far right

Opens the broader calendar and scheduling view.

Personal calendar information can contribute availability to Roundhouse without exposing private event details to an employer or Manager.

---

# 2. Trade Team Member Command Center

The Trade Team Member uses the same basic Command Center structure as the Trade Professional:

- four-area top bar;
- personal visual Timeline and Timeline Search;
- four right-edge tabs;
- five bottom controls.

Their Timeline is their own attributed participation. Other workers do not crowd it.

The current Business logo/name opens the Business Entity page according to the Team Member's permissions. Property access and working controls are likewise determined by Role, assignment, management authority, and permission.

A Trade Team Member can use their personal Timeline to search their own historical work across Properties without receiving broader access to those Properties or other workers' records.

---

# 3. Homeowner Command Center

Upon completing Intake or signing in, the Homeowner lands on **one personal Command Center**. Roundhouse does not create a separate personal Command Center for every Property the Homeowner owns.

The Homeowner Command Center is the working view across the Homeowner's relevant residential Properties. It is not itself the permanent Property record.

The Timeline can assemble the Homeowner's attributable and relevant activity across those Properties. Home Team Members, family members, and Trade Professionals do not automatically flood the Homeowner's personal Timeline merely because they participate in the same Property.

## Single-Property Homeowner

Most Homeowners will have one Property. Roundhouse should make that common case feel simple rather than presenting unnecessary account-management language.

When the Homeowner has only one residential Property, the top of the Command Center can present that Property's image and name in the Entity identity area. The Command Center therefore feels naturally centered on the Home while remaining the Homeowner's personal working screen.

The architectural distinction still remains:

**Homeowner Command Center = the Homeowner's working view.**  
**Property Entity = the permanent record of the Home.**

If the Homeowner later adds a second Property, the same Command Center expands into the multi-Property state below.

## Multi-Property Homeowner

When the Homeowner owns or manages multiple residential Properties, the top of the Command Center must clearly stop implying that one Property represents the whole Command Center.

The Entity identity area should instead identify the combined state with wording such as:

**Multi-Property Account**

The final customer-facing wording can be refined, but the meaning must be clear: the Homeowner is looking across multiple Properties.

The Homeowner Timeline may interweave activity from those Properties chronologically, with each item clearly identifying which Property it belongs to. Lists, Daily Grind, Receipts, Calendar, and other appropriate Command Center working views can likewise assemble relevant information across the Homeowner's Properties.

The Command Center does not merge those Property records. It is simply the Homeowner's working view across them.

## Top Bar — Four Areas

### 1. Avatar Photo — far left

Tap to open the Avatar/Role menu and access appropriate Role/profile information and settings.

### 2. Property / Multi-Property Identity — beside Avatar

For a Homeowner with one Property, this area shows the **Property image + Property name**.

For a Homeowner with multiple Properties, this area shows the **combined multi-Property identity** rather than pretending that one Property is selected. A temporary governing label is **Multi-Property Account**.

From the multi-Property state, the Properties working tab provides the direct path to select a specific Property.

### 3. Badge + Points — toward the right

Shows the Homeowner Avatar's status and points. Tap to open Rewards.

### 4. Inbox — far right

Combined messages and notifications.

---

## Homeowner Timeline and Search

The Homeowner uses the same visual Timeline model and Today-first hierarchy as the Trade Professional.

For a one-Property Homeowner, the Command Center naturally reflects activity associated with that Homeowner and Home.

For a multi-Property Homeowner, the Command Center Timeline can display the Homeowner's relevant activity across multiple Properties while clearly labeling the Property context of each item.

The magnifying-glass icon remains in the upper-left corner of the Timeline. Tap it to expand:

**Search your Timeline…**

Search filters the Homeowner's current Command Center Timeline.

To search the permanent collective history of one Property, the Homeowner enters that Property and searches its Property Timeline.

---

## Homeowner Right Edge — Four Tabs

The same four right-edge icon/handle tabs remain available according to the Homeowner's access:

1. Daily Grind
2. Tasks / Lists
3. Receipts
4. Properties

Daily Grind may include household work, personal items, Property schedules, assignments, reminders, calendar information, and Resolutions relevant today.

The **Properties** tab is especially important for multi-Property Homeowners because it provides the working doorway from the combined Command Center into one specific Property.

---

## Homeowner Bottom Bar — Five Controls

The same five primarily icon-based controls remain across the bottom:

1. Resolution Toggle
2. People
3. CAPTURE
4. Estimates / Invoices
5. Calendar

Controls may adapt to the Homeowner Role rather than exposing Trade-only actions that do not apply.

---

# 4. Home Team Member Command Center

The Home Team Member uses the same basic physical Command Center layout.

Their Timeline remains personal: their own chores, projects, photographs, lists, accomplishments, assignments, points, and other participation. It is not the entire Property Timeline.

The Property image/name opens the Property Entity according to permission. The Property Entity shows the shared history of what is happening at the home.

Home Team Members can earn points for legitimate household contribution and participation without turning the Property Timeline into their personal scoreboard.

---

# 5. Property Entity Destination

The Property Entity is **not another Command Center**.

It is the persistent digital place and permanent record for one Property.

When a Homeowner is in a multi-Property Command Center and deliberately selects one Property, the visual transition must be obvious. The Property Entity begins with a recognizable **Property splash/hero area at the top** so the person immediately understands that the scope has narrowed to this one place.

Its defining presentation is:

**Back → Large Property Photo → Property Name + Physical Address → Property Documents → Property Timeline**

The large Property photograph, name, and address make the physical location immediately recognizable, particularly for workers moving between jobs.

Once inside the Property, the Timeline, Lists, Documents, participants, and other Property-specific views concern that Property rather than the Homeowner's combined multi-Property Command Center.

For example:

**Homeowner Command Center → Properties → Spring Lake → Spring Lake splash/hero → Spring Lake Timeline / Lists / Records**

Activity from another Property such as Dripping Springs does not remain mixed into the Spring Lake Property view.

For a single-Property Homeowner, the Command Center may already show the home's identity at the top for simplicity. Choosing **View Property** still creates the clear splash/hero transition into the permanent Property Entity.

## Property Documents

Property Documents is the durable document repository for information that belongs with the Property rather than in a person's Command Center.

It can contain items such as:

- Warranties
- Manuals
- Disclaimers
- Water reports
- Soil reports
- Surveys
- Easements
- Inspections
- Permits
- Other Property-specific reports and documents

Documents remain connected to the underlying Record, work, equipment, condition, or event that produced them where applicable. Property Documents provides a predictable retrieval point without duplicating the information into permanent Command Center controls.

Accepted Disclaimers are persistent Property Records. When a Disclaimer remains materially relevant to authorized work, it cannot simply be hidden from an authorized Trade Professional whose work may be affected by it. A later Record may resolve or supersede the condition without erasing the historical Disclaimer.

Property Documents persist with the Property Entity through changes in homeowners, Trade Professionals, and other participants, subject to the governing record, authority, privacy, and visibility rules.

The Property Entity's Timeline remains the collective chronological history of the Property.

A Homeowner primarily enters it to see and manage the shared story of the home. A Trade Professional may enter the same Property Entity to perform legitimate work and receives the working controls appropriate to their Role and permissions.

It is the same underlying Property Entity and the same underlying Property history.

---

# 6. Business Entity Destination

The Business Entity is also separate from the personal Command Center and deliberately limited.

Its defining presentation is:

**Back → Large Company Photograph → Business Identity → Business Timeline**

The Business Timeline is collective company activity according to viewer permissions.

Command Center tools such as People, Properties, Estimates/Invoices, Calendar, and personal Daily Grind should not be duplicated onto the Business Entity merely because they exist in the Owner's personal Command Center.

The Business Entity receives only information and controls that genuinely belong at the Business level.

---

# Governing Command Center Rules

1. **Personal Command Centers belong to Avatars.**
2. **The Timeline is the visual and architectural centerpiece of the Command Center.**
3. **Avatar Timeline = what I am doing.**
4. **Entity Timeline = what is happening here.**
5. A Homeowner has one personal Command Center, not one personal Command Center per Property.
6. A single-Property Homeowner Command Center may visually identify itself with that Property because this is the common/simple case.
7. A multi-Property Homeowner Command Center must clearly identify itself as a combined view rather than implying that one Property is selected.
8. Entering one Property narrows the scope and introduces the large Property splash/hero at the top of the Property Entity.
9. Tapping the current Entity identity leaves the personal Command Center and opens the separate Entity page when a single Entity is represented there.
10. Entity pages use a large representative image and a clear Back arrow so the person always understands the change in context.
11. Coworker, subordinate, family, and Trade activity does not automatically flood another person's personal Timeline.
12. Management authority grants legitimate visibility and control; it does not merge another person's Timeline into the Manager's Timeline.
13. Search filters the Timeline currently being viewed and never grants additional permission.
14. Working tools belong primarily in the Command Center. Durable Property information belongs primarily with the Property Entity.
15. Property Documents belongs on the Property Entity, not as another permanent Command Center button.
16. Notes, photographs, documents, and other records should not receive duplicate navigation merely because they can appear in Timeline history; add dedicated access only where there is a genuine retrieval or workflow need.
17. The same underlying Record may appear in appropriate Avatar and Entity Timeline views without creating duplicate records.
18. **Entities are where interaction happens. Entity Timelines record shared history. Avatar Timelines assemble each person's individual history from their attributed participation across those Entities.**
