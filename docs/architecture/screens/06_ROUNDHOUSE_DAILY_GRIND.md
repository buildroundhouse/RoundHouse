# ROUNDHOUSE — DAILY GRIND

## Purpose

Daily Grind is the Command Center’s **day-planning workspace**. It answers:

**What am I doing today, what do I need to get done, and what should I prepare for next?**

It combines the pieces that matter to the day—appointments, tasks, reminders, unfinished work, shopping needs, relevant Resolutions, personal commitments, weather, and a short look at tomorrow—without duplicating the systems those items come from.

## When It Opens

On the first sign-in of the day, Daily Grind can open automatically with a short Concierge greeting:

> **Good morning, Daniel. I’m looking forward to helping you with your day.** It’s 72° with a high of 84°, with possible rain this afternoon. You have a 10:00 appointment at 110 Dripping Springs. It’s also your day to pick up **Martina** after school and take her to dance practice.

During the day, it becomes **what’s next and what’s unfinished**. Later, it naturally becomes a place to prepare for tomorrow.

## Visual

Daily Grind opens like a **large sheet sliding over the Command Center**, covering roughly 90% of it. It covers the top Profile/Entity/Rewards/Mail area and the Timeline, reaching nearly to the left edge while leaving just a clipped hint of Search underneath.

The **right-side tabs and bottom buttons remain exposed**, making it obvious the person never left the Command Center. The center **CAPTURE/Concierge button dips upward into Daily Grind**, just as it normally dips into the Timeline.

Inside is a clean day-oriented layout with **Today dominant** and a small **Yesterday | Today | Tomorrow** strip for context.

**Calendar schedules time. Daily Grind runs the day.**
