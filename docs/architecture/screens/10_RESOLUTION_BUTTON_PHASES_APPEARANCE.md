# Resolution Button Phases Appearance

**Same centered toggle. Different states. Clear at a glance.**

This document is the visual-appearance reference for the five active Resolution toggle phases. It governs the physical read and escalation treatment of the control; Resolution workflow behavior remains governed by `09_ROUNDHOUSE_RESOLUTION_CENTER.md`.

## Core Toggle Geometry

The Resolution control is **not a slider**.

- The pivot is fixed at the exact geometric center of a narrow horizontal pill-shaped placard.
- A short cylindrical post pivots from that fixed center point.
- The post has no centered/upright state; it leans fully left or fully right.
- Viewed straight down, the colored circular tip moves almost into the corresponding rounded end of the pill without crossing the edge.
- A very thin crescent of gray placard remains visible around the outside of the colored circular tip.
- The entire top face of the post is a flat signal color: green or red. It is not a dot.
- The colored face has no gloss and no gradient.
- The shaft remains roughly consistent in diameter rather than tapering dramatically toward the pivot.
- Only enough light gray/white shading should appear on the shaft to communicate that it is cylindrical and leaning.
- The lower pill placard is essentially flat gray, with almost no dimensional rendering.
- The overall visual language is a clean emoji/interface symbol rather than realistic industrial hardware.
- The centered pivot must remain visually obvious in every state so the control never reads as a horizontally sliding element.

## Five Active Appearance Stages

| Stage | Label | Toggle Position | Tip | Base | Escalation Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | **Waiting on Them** | Leans fully left from the fixed center pivot | Green | Gray | No colored rim |
| **2** | **Waiting on You** | Leans fully right from the fixed center pivot | Red | Gray | No colored rim |
| **3** | **Follow-Up 1** | Leans fully right from the fixed center pivot | Red | Gray | Yellow rim around the placard |
| **4** | **Follow-Up 2** | Leans fully right from the fixed center pivot | Red | Gray | Red rim around the placard |
| **5** | **Urgent** | Leans fully right from the fixed center pivot | Red | Gray | Placard is on fire |

## Signal Logic

**Green = waiting on them.**  
**Red = your turn / needs your attention.**

Repeated unanswered follow-up escalates the **placard**, not the toggle itself:

**Normal → Yellow rim → Red rim → Fire**

The pivot, shaft geometry, lean direction, and circular tip relationship do not change during escalation. Only the signal treatment changes.

## Closed Resolutions

Closed Resolution appearance is governed separately by the Resolution Center architecture. Closed Resolutions become gray and can remain **Resolved — New/Unread** until the other participant opens the final outcome. The closed state is not one of the five active escalation phases shown here.
