# ROUNDHOUSE — PROPERTIES WORKING FUNCTION

## Purpose

**Properties is the Command Center working doorway into the Properties a person currently owns, manages, or is working within.**

It is not a second Property Entity page, a second Timeline, or a client database.

The Command Center works across the person's relevant Properties. Entering a Property narrows Roundhouse to that one place.

**Command Center = my activity across the Properties I manage or work within.**  
**Property = the permanent record of this one place.**

The Command Center is a working view. **It is not the permanent record. The Property Entity is.**

## Position in the Command Center

Properties is the **fourth and lowest tab on the right-side working rail**:

1. Daily Grind
2. Tasks / Lists
3. Receipts
4. **Properties**

Properties operates like the other right-side working functions.

Tapping Properties opens a large working sheet over the Command Center rather than immediately navigating to another screen. The sheet covers the upper/main working surface and Timeline while preserving the right-side tool rail and bottom Command Center control bar.

The center **CAPTURE/Concierge** control continues to protrude upward into the sheet. A thin clipped hint of the underlying Search/Command Center may remain visible at the left edge so the person understands they are still inside the Command Center.

Tapping another right-side tab swaps the working sheet without first closing Properties.

## What Appears

The Properties sheet should be simple and recognizable.

It shows the Properties relevant to the person's current Roundhouse working context, with current/open Properties given priority.

Each Property should be recognizable primarily by:

- Property photo
- Property name
- Address
- appropriate owner/client identification where useful

The large recognizable Property image is important. People working across several homes or jobs should be able to identify a place visually without reading a database-style record.

Search provides access to additional Properties the person legitimately has access to without cluttering the active working view.

## Homeowner Command Center

A Homeowner Command Center can represent work and activity across **all Properties that homeowner owns or manages in that Homeowner context**.

For example, if a homeowner owns two houses, both Properties can appear in the Properties working function.

Their personal Command Center Timeline can interweave attributable/relevant activity from both Properties because the Command Center answers:

**What is happening in my world?**

The same applies to Lists. From the Command Center, the homeowner can see their personal lists and Property-associated lists across the Properties they manage, subject to permissions.

The homeowner does not have to enter each Property simply to understand their overall day or current activity.

## Trade Professional Command Center

A Trade Professional's Properties sheet shows the Properties/jobs where that Trade Professional is currently working or has relevant open work.

Their Command Center Timeline can similarly reflect their own attributable activity across multiple jobs.

This does not merge the Properties themselves. It is simply the Trade Professional's personal working view across those contexts.

## Commercial Property and Acting Context

A person may have more than one legitimate Roundhouse acting context.

For example, the same person might own two residential Properties and also have management authority over a commercial Property.

The commercial Property remains its own Property Entity with its own authority structure. If entering or acting within it requires a Commercial Management / Facility Management context, Roundhouse should make that acting-context change explicit rather than silently treating Homeowner authority as commercial management authority.

The person's identity remains the same. Their acting Role/authority determines how they participate in that Entity.

## Entering a Property

The Properties working sheet is the doorway. Entering the actual Property changes the scope.

If the homeowner enters **Spring Lake**, Roundhouse stops presenting the combined view across Spring Lake and Dripping Springs and narrows to Spring Lake.

Now the person sees information belonging to or appropriately associated with **that Property**:

- Spring Lake Timeline
- Spring Lake lists available to that person
- Spring Lake documents
- Spring Lake participants
- Spring Lake Records and other Property-specific functions

Activity from Dripping Springs does not remain mixed into the Spring Lake Property view.

Likewise, when a Trade Professional enters 110 Dripping Springs, the view narrows from that Trade Professional's multi-job Command Center to the context of 110 Dripping Springs.

## Property Entity Boundary

The Properties tab and Property Entity must not become duplicates.

**Properties working sheet = choose and work with a Property from the Command Center.**  
**Property Entity = the actual persistent digital place and permanent Property record.**

The Properties working sheet should therefore stay lean. It should not recreate full Notes, Lists, Receipts, Calendar, Resolutions, Documents, or Timeline systems inside itself.

Those systems retain their own homes and can be filtered/narrowed by Property context.

A deliberate **View Property** action can take the person from the Command Center working sheet into the full Property Entity when they want the permanent Property view.

## Property Context Does Not Mean Property Visibility

Entering or selecting a Property does not mean every piece of information created while working there becomes part of the homeowner-visible Property record.

A Trade Professional or Business can have an internal:

- Note
- Reminder
- List
- Receipt
- working record

associated with 110 Dripping Springs while that information remains private to the Trade Professional/Business according to permissions.

This information still belongs in the **context** of 110 Dripping Springs because that is where the work occurred. But it does not automatically become part of the homeowner's Property record.

**The Property tells Roundhouse where the work belongs. It does not automatically determine who can see every Record created there.**

Only information deliberately contributed to or shared with the Property record becomes homeowner-visible according to the Property's permissions and Record rules.

## Concierge

Because Properties begins as a Command Center working sheet, an already-active Concierge remains available there.

This allows work-context interactions such as finding a Property, locating something previously documented there, or adding an item to the appropriate Property-related working list without creating a separate Concierge interface.

When the person deliberately leaves the Command Center working sheet and enters another experience, the existing Concierge boundary rules apply.

## Governing Rules

1. **Properties is the fourth and lowest right-side Command Center working tab.**
2. It opens as the same large working-sheet pattern used by Daily Grind, Tasks / Lists, and Receipts.
3. The right-side rail, bottom controls, and CAPTURE/Concierge remain available while the Properties sheet is open.
4. The Command Center can assemble a person's relevant activity across multiple Properties.
5. The Command Center is a working view, **not the permanent Property record**.
6. Entering a Property narrows the view to that single Property.
7. A Property Entity maintains its own Timeline, Records, documents, participants, and other Property-specific information.
8. Homeowners can use one Command Center across the Properties they own/manage within the appropriate context.
9. Trade Professionals can use one Command Center across the Properties/jobs where they are working.
10. Different authority contexts, including commercial/facility management, must remain explicit rather than silently inheriting Homeowner authority.
11. Property context does not automatically make internal Business information homeowner-visible.
12. **Avatar Timeline = what I am doing across my contexts. Property Timeline = what is happening at this Property.**
