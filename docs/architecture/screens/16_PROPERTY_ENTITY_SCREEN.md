# Roundhouse Property Entity Screen

## Purpose

The Property Entity is the permanent digital place for one Property and also a legitimate workplace for authorized Homeowners, Home Team Members, Viewers, Trade Professionals, Trade Team Members, Commercial participants, and other explicitly authorized people.

It is not another personal Command Center.

**Command Center = the person's working view.**  
**Property Entity = what is happening here, what belongs here, and what authorized people can do here.**

The Property Entity preserves useful positional familiarity from the Command Center while remaining visually distinct enough that the person always understands they are inside a Property.

Participation and invitation behavior is governed by **`33_INVITATIONS.md`** and **`../ROUNDHOUSE_ROLES_AND_PERMISSIONS.md`**.

---

## 1. Top Identity Area

The person's Avatar remains visible in the upper-right so the interface always identifies who is operating.

The Entity view does **not** show the person's Points / Status control. Points belong to the person and remain part of the personal Command Center / Reward Center experience.

An Entity-context Mail control remains available. Opening Mail from the Property Entity opens the same Roundhouse Mail system filtered to conversations associated with that Property and available to the current person.

Notifications remain available beside Mail according to **`32_NOTIFICATIONS.md`**.

---

## 2. Compact Property Header

The Property Entity begins with a compact horizontal identity header.

### Left approximately one-third

Shows:

- Property name
- Physical address

If the person has access to multiple Properties, a small bright Roundhouse-blue **Switch** control appears beneath the Property identity.

**Switch** changes the current Property context among Properties the person legitimately has access to.

### Right approximately two-thirds

Shows the representative Property photograph / splash image.

---

## 3. Property Timeline

The Property Timeline remains the visual and architectural center of the Entity screen.

It shows the collective chronological history of the Property according to the current person's Role, authority, permissions, and legitimate historical access.

The Timeline Search control filters the Property Timeline only.

**Avatar Timeline = what I am doing.**  
**Property Timeline = what is happening here.**

The living Timeline and durable **Vault → Property History / Records** may present the same underlying Record without duplication.

When a person has newly received legitimate access, the Property Entity may show **Property Handoff available**. Handoff remains governed by **`25_PROPERTY_HANDOFF.md`**.

---

# 4. Property Participation and Access

The Property Entity must always be able to explain **why a current person has access**.

Typical permission sources include:

- direct Owner authorization;
- delegated Property Manager authorization;
- Business-derived assignment;
- Commercial Management authorization;
- Viewer invitation;
- independent direct Property relationship.

The permission source is not merely audit metadata. It determines whether access should continue when an upstream relationship ends.

## Viewer

**Viewer is the neutral view-only Role.**

A neutral Viewer profile does not create a relationship by itself. The person must be invited or otherwise legitimately authorized to this specific Residential Property or Commercial Facility.

Viewer receives only the explicitly granted view scope and does not gain:

- CAPTURE contribution authority;
- Work assignment authority;
- participant-management authority;
- estimate / invoice authority;
- edit authority;
- broader access to unrelated Properties or Businesses.

There is no Trade Viewer Role.

---

# 5. Adding Trade Participants to a Claimed Property

A Trade Business may bring an accepted subcontractor / outside Trade Professional forward for Property participation.

The normal sequence is:

**Business invites participant → participant accepts Business relationship → Business assigns / proposes participant to Property → Property approval is resolved**

For a claimed Property where the Business does not hold delegated participant-management authority, the Homeowner receives a simple approval request.

The Homeowner should see:

- Business;
- participant;
- intended Role / relationship;
- Property;
- Work / job context where applicable;
- requested permission scope;
- person who initiated the request.

The Homeowner does **not** need to search for the subcontractor, invite them manually, or ask them to request access independently.

---

# 6. Delegated Manager Authority

A Homeowner may explicitly delegate **Manager authority** that includes permission to manage Property participants.

When that permission is active, an authorized Trade Professional / Business Manager can approve an appropriate Business participant into this Property on the Owner's behalf.

The flow becomes:

**Business assigns accepted participant → Manager approves within delegated scope → participant receives Property access → Homeowner is informed**

The Homeowner receives an informational Notification rather than a redundant approval task.

Example:

**JD Design Studios added Carlos Hernandez to Spring Lake Residence.**  
**Authorized through JD's Manager authority.**

The Property history must preserve:

- who exercised the authority;
- the Business relationship;
- the delegated authority source;
- participant;
- permission scope;
- date / time.

Removing or narrowing Manager authority stops future approvals outside the remaining scope.

---

# 7. Business-Derived Access

When a Trade participant's Property access exists because they are participating through a Trade Business, that access is **Business-derived**.

If the governing Business relationship ends, Roundhouse automatically removes current Property permissions that depend solely on that Business relationship.

The Homeowner should not have to remove a former subcontractor manually from every affected Property.

This revocation does not erase legitimate:

- Work;
- CAPTURE evidence;
- Timeline activity;
- communications;
- Resolutions;
- Property History;
- attribution.

**Current access ends. Historical truth remains.**

## Independent Reconnection

If the Homeowner independently wants to keep working with that Trade Professional, the Homeowner may establish a new direct Property relationship.

That independent authorization has its own permission source and survives unrelated Business changes.

---

# 8. Unclaimed Property

Before the legitimate Owner has claimed the Property in Roundhouse, a legitimate managing Trade Business may create and manage the participants necessary to perform and document work within its operational authority.

Roundhouse must not create a circular flow where the Business assigns a participant, the participant requests access back to the same Business-managed Property, and the Business then approves its own request.

The same Property Record remains when the legitimate Owner later claims it. Existing history remains; current permissions are reevaluated under Owner governance.

---

# 9. Right Edge — Four Property Tabs

The Property Entity uses the same four-tab right-edge geometry as the Command Center:

1. **Work**
2. **Tasks / Lists**
3. **Maintenance**
4. **Vault**

## Work

Work answers:

**What needs doing at this place?**

It covers the governing lifecycle:

**Requested → Open → Assigned → In Progress → Complete → Verified**

with Cancelled as a terminal exception.

Business Work and Property Work reference the same underlying Work Record where appropriate.

**Work organizes the job. CAPTURE documents the real work session.**

Full behavior: **`23_PROPERTY_WORK.md`**.

## Tasks / Lists

Tasks / Lists handles lightweight Property organization:

**Tasks | Checklists | Lists**

Items that become real jobs can deliberately **Convert to Work**.

Private personal or Business-internal items can be associated with the Property without automatically becoming Property-visible.

Full behavior: **`24_PROPERTY_TASKS_LISTS.md`**.

## Maintenance

Maintenance contains:

**Routine Maintenance | Standards**

Due care or Standards drift can generate Work rather than creating a competing execution system.

Full behavior: **`18_PROPERTY_MAINTENANCE_STANDARDS.md`**.

## Vault

Vault is the permanent Property-information destination.

Primary sections:

- **Specs**
- **Assets & Equipment**
- **Documents**
- **Property History / Records**

Property Handoff remains a persistent secondary Vault-header action beside Search Vault, not a fifth section.

Full behavior:

- **`19_PROPERTY_ASSET_DETAIL.md`**
- **`20_PROPERTY_VAULT_SPECS.md`**
- **`21_PROPERTY_VAULT_DOCUMENTS_RECORDS.md`**
- **`22_PROPERTY_HISTORY_RECORDS.md`**
- **`25_PROPERTY_HANDOFF.md`**

---

# 10. Bottom Bar — Familiar Working Positions

From left to right:

1. **Resolution**
2. **People**
3. **CAPTURE**
4. **Estimates / Invoices**
5. **Calendar**

Each operates in the current Property context and according to Role, authority, permissions, and permission source.

## People

People shows only legitimate current Property relationships.

Pending invitations do not appear as active People relationships. They remain in the Invitation Center / pending participant flow until resolved.

A Trade Professional brought through a Business may show concise relationship context such as **Through JD Design Studios** when that helps the Owner understand why the person has access.

A Viewer appears only because this Property or Facility deliberately authorized the Viewer.

## CAPTURE

CAPTURE carries the Property context into the work session automatically.

Viewer does not receive CAPTURE contribution authority.

---

# 11. Visual Distinction From the Command Center

Primary context signals include:

- compact Property-specific header;
- representative Property image;
- Property name / address;
- blue Switch when multiple Properties are available;
- Property Timeline;
- Work rather than personal Daily Grind;
- Tasks / Lists;
- Maintenance;
- Vault;
- optional first-entry Handoff prompt;
- absence of personal Points / Status.

The goal is familiar operation with unmistakable Property context.

---

# 12. Governing Rules

1. The Property Entity is both a permanent record and a legitimate place where authorized people work or view according to Role.
2. Viewer is the neutral view-only Role and only becomes meaningful through a specific Residential Property or Commercial Facility authorization.
3. There is no free-floating social Property relationship and no Trade Viewer Role.
4. Every active Property permission retains a source.
5. A Trade Business brings its own subcontractor / outside Trade Professional forward rather than making the participant independently negotiate Property access.
6. Claimed Property participant additions require Owner approval unless delegated Manager authority explicitly grants participant-management authority.
7. A Manager approval is attributable to the Manager and Business that exercised the delegated authority; the Owner receives an informational Notification.
8. A legitimate managing Business may establish necessary operational participant access on an Unclaimed Property without a circular request / approval loop.
9. Business-derived Property access automatically ends when the governing Business relationship ends unless another independent authorization remains.
10. Independent Property authorization survives unrelated Business changes.
11. Permission changes never erase legitimate historical attribution or Records.
12. Pending invitations are not active People relationships.
13. Property Work, Tasks / Lists, Maintenance, and Vault retain their established governing responsibilities.
14. Mail opened from Property is the same Roundhouse Mail filtered to this Entity.
15. Property Timeline is the living view; Vault → Property History / Records is the durable archival view.
16. Handoff assembles existing permitted Property truth rather than duplicating it.

---

## Governing Phrase

**The Command Center tells me what I am doing. The Property Entity tells me what is happening here, why I have access, and gives me only the tools I am authorized to use here.**
