# Roundhouse Business Properties

## Purpose

**Properties** is the third right-edge working screen inside the Business Entity.

Its home is:

**Business Entity → Properties**

It answers:

**Where is this Business legitimately authorized to work?**

Business Properties is not the Business owner's personal Property list, not a lead list, and not every Property the Business has ever heard about.

A Property appears here because the Business has a legitimate current Property relationship.

Participant-establishment and delegated approval rules are governed by **`33_INVITATIONS.md`**.

---

# 1. Position Inside the Business Entity

The Business Entity right-edge order remains:

1. **Work**
2. **Team**
3. **Properties**
4. **Vault**

The visible label is **Properties**.

---

# 2. Governing Property Eligibility

A Property appears in Business → Properties only when the Business has a legitimate current relationship to that Property.

Examples include:

- Owner-authorized service relationship;
- accepted Work;
- ongoing maintenance responsibility;
- explicit Property participation;
- delegated Manager relationship;
- legitimate Business-created Property awaiting Owner claim;
- another explicit Roundhouse authorization path.

A past lead, rejected estimate, imported address, personal contact, or old customer name does not automatically become an active Business Property.

---

# 3. Two Primary Property Sections

The default screen is divided into:

## Owner-Connected

Properties where a Homeowner / Owner has claimed governance and the Business has a legitimate authorized relationship.

## Unclaimed

Properties the Business has legitimately created or is operationally maintaining, but no Owner has yet claimed governance in Roundhouse.

The visible user-facing label is simply:

**Unclaimed**

This is an ownership / governance distinction, not a Work-status distinction.

---

# 4. Claim State Is Not Work Status

A Property can be Owner-Connected with no current Work.

An Unclaimed Property can have active Work, Maintenance, Documents, Assets, Timeline history, or participants.

Claim state must not be confused with job state.

---

# 5. Claim Transition

When the legitimate Owner claims an Unclaimed Property, Roundhouse does **not** create a new Property.

The same Property transitions:

**Unclaimed → Owner-Connected**

The Property preserves legitimate existing:

- Work;
- CAPTURE evidence;
- Maintenance;
- Assets;
- Specs;
- Documents;
- Property History;
- Business relationships;
- participant history;
- Timeline records.

The claim process may change current permissions and approval rules, but it does not rewrite historical truth.

---

# 6. Business Authority on Unclaimed Properties

An Unclaimed Property does not mean the Business owns the real estate.

It means Roundhouse does not yet have a claimed Owner identity governing that Property.

A legitimate managing Business may have enough operational authority to create and maintain necessary Property records and assign accepted Business participants to the Property.

Roundhouse should avoid a circular flow where the Business assigns its subcontractor, the subcontractor requests access back to the same Business-managed Property, and the Business then approves the request.

Instead:

**Business manages Unclaimed Property → Business assigns accepted participant → appropriate Business-derived Property access is established directly.**

---

# 7. Owner-Connected Property Participant Approval

For a claimed Property, the Business cannot automatically place people into the Homeowner's Entity merely because those people belong to the Business.

The normal path is:

**Business assigns / proposes accepted participant → Homeowner reviews → Approve / Decline**

The approval request identifies:

- Business;
- participant;
- Property;
- Work / job context where applicable;
- requested permission scope;
- person who initiated the request.

The Homeowner does not need to find or invite the subcontractor themselves.

---

# 8. Delegated Manager Authority

A Homeowner may explicitly delegate **Manager authority** to a Trade Professional / Business and include permission to manage Property participants.

When that authority is active, an authorized Manager can approve an appropriate accepted Business participant into the Property on the Owner's behalf.

The flow becomes:

**Business assigns accepted participant → Manager approval under delegated authority → Property access active → Homeowner receives informational Notification**

Example Notification:

**JD Design Studios added Carlos Hernandez to Spring Lake Residence.**  
**Authorized through JD's Manager authority.**

The action must remain permanently attributable to the person who exercised the delegated authority.

Removing or narrowing Manager authority stops future automatic approvals outside the remaining scope.

---

# 9. Permission Source

Every Business-associated Property permission must retain its source.

Useful permission-source categories include:

- Owner direct authorization;
- delegated Property Manager authorization;
- Business-derived assignment;
- direct independent Property authorization;
- Commercial Management authorization;
- another explicit governing path.

Roundhouse must be able to answer:

**Why does this Business or person have current access to this Property?**

---

# 10. Business-Derived Participant Access

A Business participant may receive Property access because they are working through that Business.

That access is **Business-derived**.

If the governing Business relationship ends, Roundhouse automatically removes current Property permissions that depend solely on that Business relationship.

Example:

**Carlos works through JD Design Studios → JD has Carlos assigned to Spring Lake → JD removes Carlos from its outside-trade roster → Carlos's JD-derived Spring Lake access ends automatically.**

The Homeowner should not have to manually clean up JD's former participant.

Historical Work, CAPTURE evidence, Timeline activity, communications, Resolutions, and attribution remain intact.

---

# 11. Independent Property Relationships

A Business-derived relationship and an independent Property relationship are different permission sources.

If the Homeowner independently wants to continue working with a subcontractor after the original Business relationship ends, the Homeowner may establish a new direct Property authorization with that Trade Professional.

Ending the Business relationship removes only the Business-derived permission source.

It does not destroy an independently authorized relationship.

---

# 12. Properties Screen

Properties opens as a Business-scoped working sheet over the Business Timeline.

At the top:

**Properties**  
Business name beneath it  
**Search Properties**

The default body is:

**Owner-Connected**  
[property cards]

**Unclaimed**  
[property cards]

Each section may collapse while preserving its count.

---

# 13. Default Sorting

Within each section, Properties are sorted by **most recent legitimate Property activity**.

Useful activity may include recent Work, upcoming appointment, CAPTURE session, Property update, Maintenance activity, or another legitimate Business interaction tied to that Property.

---

# 14. Property Cards

Cards remain compact and easy to scan.

A card may show:

- representative Property photo;
- Property name;
- address;
- Owner / Homeowner identity where permitted;
- claim state;
- small current Work / attention indicator;
- recent activity cue;
- delegated Manager indicator when operationally important.

Example:

**Spring Lake Residence**  
110 Dripping Springs  
Owner: Sarah Miller  
JD: Manager authority · 2 active Work items

Example:

**Canyon Ridge House**  
214 Canyon Ridge  
**Unclaimed**  
Last worked Sept. 10

The card should not become another Work dashboard.

---

# 15. Property Action Card

Tapping a Property opens the established compact Property Action Card.

Primary actions:

- **Enter Property**
- **Navigate** when an authorized usable address exists

Additional contextual actions may include **Manage Participants** only when the current person's Property authority actually permits it.

Entering the Property never expands permissions beyond what the person already holds.

---

# 16. Search

**Search Properties** searches Owner-Connected and Unclaimed Properties available through the Business.

Search may match Property name, address, Owner where permitted, Work title, Property identifier, Asset, or other useful context.

Results preserve claim state and do not expose unrelated private information.

---

# 17. Relationship to Business Work

Business Properties answers:

**Where does this Business have authorized Property relationships?**

Business Work answers:

**What does this Business have on its plate across those Properties?**

A Property card may show a small active-Work count or attention state, but detailed Work remains in Business → Work.

---

# 18. Relationship to Team

Business Team establishes who operates through the Business.

Properties determines where the Business is authorized to work.

Assignment of a Team member / outside Trade Professional to a Property does not automatically mean unrestricted Property access. The Property permission path must still resolve through Owner authorization, delegated Manager authority, or legitimate Unclaimed-Property stewardship.

---

# 19. Relationship to People

A Property may connect the Business to Homeowners, occupants, Property contacts, suppliers, or other legitimate people.

Those broader relationships belong in **People**.

Properties should show only enough person context to identify and operate at the Property.

---

# 20. Relationship to Property Entity

Business Properties is a doorway, not a substitute for the Property Entity.

Entering a Property opens the Property's own Timeline, Work, Tasks / Lists, Maintenance, Vault, People, Resolution, Mail, CAPTURE, Estimates / Invoices, and Calendar according to current permission.

The Business does not receive broader Property visibility merely by entering it.

---

# 21. Inactive / Historical Relationships

When a Business no longer has an active operational relationship to a Property, that Property should not remain mixed into the active Properties screen indefinitely.

Historical Business Records remain retrievable according to retention and permission rules.

Ending current authorization does not erase legitimate historical Business records.

---

# 22. Permissions

Business Properties is permission-aware.

- Business Owners / authorized Managers may see the Business's legitimate Property relationships according to authority;
- Trade Team Members see only Properties relevant to their permitted participation;
- Outside Trade Professionals see only Properties actually authorized / assigned to them;
- a Business relationship to one Property does not imply access to another;
- a person's membership in the Business does not automatically grant Property access;
- Viewer access is granted by the Residential Property or Commercial Facility itself, not as a Business Team relationship.

---

# 23. Visual Direction

Properties should feel like a clean portfolio of authorized jobsites, not a CRM and not a map application.

Visual priorities:

- grouped-roofline right-edge icon;
- one Search bar across both sections;
- clear Owner-Connected / Unclaimed separation;
- most-recently-active sorting;
- compact photo-led Property cards;
- minimal Work indicators;
- clear claim-state language;
- delegated Manager state visible only when useful;
- no legal-ownership implication for Business-managed Unclaimed Properties.

---

## Governing Relationship

**Owner-Connected = Property has a claimed Owner and Business participation is governed by that Property authority.**  
**Unclaimed = Business has a legitimate operational Property relationship but no Owner has yet claimed governance in Roundhouse.**  
**Business-derived access = participant permission exists because of a Business relationship and ends when that governing relationship ends.**  
**Independent Property access = separately authorized permission that survives unrelated Business changes.**

---

## Governing Rule

**Business Properties shows only Properties where the Business has a legitimate current operational relationship. Claimed Properties require Owner approval for Business participants unless delegated Manager authority explicitly permits participant approval on the Owner's behalf. Unclaimed Properties allow the legitimate managing Business to establish necessary operational access directly. Every permission retains its source so Business-derived access can end automatically without erasing history or destroying independently authorized Property relationships.**
