# ROUNDHOUSE — CALENDAR

## Purpose

**Calendar is Roundhouse’s scheduling and dispatch workspace.**

It answers:

**Where is work scheduled, at what Property, at what time, and who is assigned?**

Calendar opens as a dedicated page with a **back arrow** to the Command Center.

The governing distinction is:

**Calendar schedules the work. Daily Grind runs the day.**

---

## Manager Scheduling Board

For a Trade Professional Owner or Manager, Calendar shows the business schedule across the Properties they legitimately manage.

Each appointment shows:

- Property / Client
- Date and time
- Expected duration
- Assigned worker or team
- Confirmation status

The Manager can build a potential schedule before everyone has approved it.

The basic scheduling view is:

**Property → Time Slot → Assigned Person / Team**

This is the place where the Manager thinks ahead across the week.

---

## Workers See the Day, Not the Company Calendar

Employees and ordinary Trade Team Members should **not see the entire company scheduling board**.

Seeing a full week of jobs can distract or overwhelm someone whose immediate responsibility is today’s work.

Confirmed assignments are instead pushed into that worker’s personal **Daily Grind**, normally the night before.

The worker experience remains:

**What am I doing today? → What comes next?**

Owners and Managers also receive their own appointments in their personal Daily Grind even though they have access to the broader scheduling board.

Private calendar commitments can block availability without exposing their details. A Manager may see:

**Unavailable**

without seeing why.

---

## Independent Workers and Subcontractors

A subcontractor or independent Trade Professional may have unrelated work outside the Manager’s business.

Assigning them to a time slot therefore creates a **proposed appointment**, not an automatic assignment.

They receive only that appointment request:

**Accept**  
**Decline**  
**Suggest Another Time**  
**Add Message**

They do not receive access to the Manager’s full Calendar.

Once accepted and otherwise approved, the appointment becomes **Confirmed** and is pushed into their Daily Grind like any other scheduled work.

---

## Homeowner Calendar and Approval

When homeowner approval is required, the homeowner receives the same lightweight appointment response:

**Accept · Decline · Suggest Another Time · Add Message**

An appointment may therefore show separate states:

**Homeowner — Approved**  
**Subcontractor — Pending**

It becomes fully **Confirmed** once all required parties accept.

The Homeowner Calendar shows only activity relevant to their Properties, such as:

- proposed and confirmed Trade Professional visits,
- subcontractor visits,
- gardener schedules,
- housekeeper schedules,
- Home Team appointments,
- other authorized Property events.

A multi-Property homeowner sees the relevant Property identified with each appointment.

They do **not** see a contractor’s other clients, internal company schedule, or another person’s private calendar.

---

## Appointment States

Keep the states simple:

**Proposed** — a potential time has been placed on the schedule.

**Pending Confirmation** — one or more required people still need to respond.

**Confirmed** — all required approvals have been received.

Normal appointment negotiation stays inside Calendar.

It should **not automatically become a Resolution**.

If scheduling develops into a genuine unresolved problem requiring persistent accountability, someone may deliberately create a Resolution.

**Scheduling belongs to Calendar. Scheduling problems may become Resolutions.**

---

## Creating an Appointment

Roundhouse should reuse context it already knows.

If scheduling begins from a Property, it already knows:

**Client + Property + Address**

The scheduler primarily chooses:

**Date → Time → Expected Duration → Assigned Person / Team**

Appointments created from Messages, Properties, Resolutions, or other Roundhouse workflows all feed the same Calendar system.

No duplicate scheduling systems.

---

## Daily Grind and Night-Before Delivery

Once tomorrow’s appointment is confirmed, Roundhouse pushes it into the appropriate personal **Daily Grind**.

This applies to:

- Owner / Manager
- Employees
- Trade Team Members
- Independent Trade Professionals
- Subcontractors

Each person sees only their own relevant work.

Roundhouse should also send a **night-before SMS** so tomorrow’s schedule does not depend on someone opening the app or allowing push notifications.

### Worker

**Roundhouse: You have 2 appointments tomorrow. 8:00 AM — Johnson Residence. 1:30 PM — Spring Lake. Open Daily Grind for details.**

### Homeowner

**Roundhouse: DMT Design Build is scheduled at your home tomorrow at 9:00 AM. Mike Rodriguez is assigned.**

### Manager

**Roundhouse: Tomorrow: 4 Properties scheduled and 3 workers assigned. Open Daily Grind for your day.**

SMS contains only information the recipient is authorized to receive.

Email may eventually be another option, but **SMS is the primary external next-day safeguard**.

The redundancy is intentional:

**Calendar holds the schedule → Daily Grind presents the day → SMS makes sure people know about tomorrow.**

---

## Appointment Changes

A confirmed appointment should never be silently rewritten.

If the date, time, assigned person, or another material detail changes, the appointment retains that history and affected people are notified.

If the new time requires approval, the appropriate participants return to **Pending Confirmation** until they accept it.

---

## Property and CAPTURE Connection

Appointments remain attached to their Property and can provide actions such as:

**View Property**  
**Navigate to Property**  
**Message Client**  
**Reschedule**

On the appointment day, the scheduling workflow naturally hands off to CAPTURE:

**Navigate → GPS Arrival → Check In → Work Session**

---

## Governing Rule

**Calendar coordinates Properties, people, and time without exposing more information than each person needs.**

The core flow is:

**Manager Builds Schedule → Appointment Proposed → Required People Confirm → Appointment Confirmed → Daily Grind + Night-Before SMS → Work Occurs**

The simplest expression of the architecture is:

**Managers need the week. Workers need the day. Homeowners need to know who is coming and when.**
