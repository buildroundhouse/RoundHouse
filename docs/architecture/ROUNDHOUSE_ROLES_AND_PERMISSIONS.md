# ROUNDHOUSE — ROLES & AUTHORITY

## Purpose

RoundHouse has four top-level Role categories:

1. **Property**
2. **Trade**
3. **Supplier**
4. **History Viewer**

Each category has sub-roles that define how the person participates and, where applicable, what authority they hold.

The **Homeowner is the center of the Residential Property experience**. Trade, Supplier, Viewer, Admin, Manager, and other supporting roles exist around that relationship.

A person’s current Role or authority may change without altering or erasing their previous Timeline activity, records, messages, Work, CAPTURE, or other legitimate contributions.

---

## 1. Property Roles

Property includes Residential Property and Commercial Property / Facility.

Residential Property uses the following Home titles:

### Homeowner
The standard unpaid homeowner role.

### Home Admin
Administrative Home role. Admin requires paid access.

### Home (Viewer)
Limited viewing role for a Home.

A Home Viewer may be paid or unpaid. Viewer access is available when either the Viewer or the Homeowner has a paid subscription.

### Home Pro (Owner)
Paid homeowner role with Owner authority.

### Home Pro (Manager)
Paid Home role with management authority.

### Home Pro (Teammate)
Paid Home role participating as part of the Home team.

Commercial Property / Facility follows the same Property authority system. Its Role and authority must reflect the person’s actual relationship to that Property without inventing a separate Commercial role system.

---

## 2. Trade Roles

### Trade Professional
Standard unpaid Trade role.

### Trade Admin
Administrative Trade role. Admin requires paid access.

### Trade Pro (Owner)
Paid Trade role with ownership authority.

### Trade Pro (Manager)
Paid Trade role with management authority.

### Trade Pro (Lead)
Paid Trade role with Lead responsibility.

### Trade Pro (Owner – Lead)
Paid Trade role combining ownership and Lead responsibility.

### Trade Pro (Manager – Lead)
Paid Trade role combining management and Lead responsibility.

There is no plain **Trade Pro** title. An unpaid working Trade account is **Trade Professional**.

---

## 3. Supplier Roles

### Supplier
Standard unpaid Supplier role.

### Supplier Admin
Administrative Supplier role. Admin requires paid access.

### Supplier Pro (Owner)
Paid Supplier role with ownership authority.

### Supplier Pro (Manager)
Paid Supplier role with management authority.

### Supplier Pro (Lead)
Paid Supplier role with Lead responsibility.

### Supplier Pro (Owner – Lead)
Paid Supplier role combining ownership and Lead responsibility.

### Supplier Pro (Manager – Lead)
Paid Supplier role combining management and Lead responsibility.

---

## 4. History Viewer

### History Viewer
The only Role available in a person’s private History.

History Viewer may be paid or unpaid.

It allows the person to view their combined legitimate history, use **Share RoundHouse**, and connect to a Property or Business.

History Viewer does not exist for a brand-new account before the person has established at least one legitimate Property or Business relationship.

# Authority

Authority determines what a person can control within their current Role.

## Paid-Party Rule

For any people-to-people interaction, organization, or coordination inside a Property, Business, or Facility, **at least one party to that relationship must have a paid subscription**.

The paid party may be either:

- the person interacting or participating, or
- the person who owns or controls the Property, Business, or Facility.

Examples:

- paid Homeowner + unpaid Home Viewer → allowed
- unpaid Homeowner + paid Home Viewer → allowed
- paid Business + unpaid participant → allowed
- unpaid Business + paid participant → allowed
- unpaid Homeowner + unpaid Viewer → shared organizational functions unavailable
- unpaid Business + unpaid participant → shared organizational functions unavailable

---

## Viewer Authority

A Viewer may:

- view the information available to them
- message where messaging is available
- use **Share RoundHouse**
- search for a Property or Business
- connect to an existing Property or Business
- create a new Property or Business within free-account limits
- operate a Property or Business they create within the limits of the free role

A Viewer may not:

- organize workers
- create or manage teams
- use paid management functions
- publicly share or publish Property or Business activity that requires paid authority

A free account may create:

- **one Property**
- **one Business**

A free account cannot proactively transfer ownership of a Property or Business it created.

Ownership transfer begins only when another person finds that Property or Business and **requests ownership transfer**.

---

## Admin Authority

Admin requires paid access.

Admin can establish and administer a Property, Trade Business, or Supplier Business without claiming ownership.

Admin may organize workers, create and manage teams, administer participants, and use paid management functions.

Admin does not automatically become the Owner.

Admin titles do not receive a separate Pro version because Admin already requires paid access.

---

## Owner Authority

Owner identifies legitimate ownership authority.

Owner appears in:

- **Home Pro (Owner)**
- **Trade Pro (Owner)**
- **Trade Pro (Owner – Lead)**
- **Supplier Pro (Owner)**
- **Supplier Pro (Owner – Lead)**

---

## Manager Authority

Manager authority allows a person to manage people, Work, schedules, or activity within their permitted scope.

Manager appears in:

- **Home Pro (Manager)**
- **Trade Pro (Manager)**
- **Trade Pro (Manager – Lead)**
- **Supplier Pro (Manager)**
- **Supplier Pro (Manager – Lead)**

---

## Lead Authority

Lead identifies primary responsibility for professional work.

Lead applies to Trade and Supplier roles, not Property roles.

Lead appears in:

- **Trade Pro (Lead)**
- **Trade Pro (Owner – Lead)**
- **Trade Pro (Manager – Lead)**
- **Supplier Pro (Lead)**
- **Supplier Pro (Owner – Lead)**
- **Supplier Pro (Manager – Lead)**

---

## Role and Authority Changes

Changing a person’s Role or authority does not alter, erase, or rewrite their previous Timeline activity, records, messages, Work, CAPTURE, or other legitimate contributions.

Historical activity remains attributed to:

- the person
- the Role they held at the time
- the authority they held at the time
- the original date and context

A Role or authority change affects current and future access only.

**Previous contributions remain part of the historical record.**

---

## Governing Rule

**RoundHouse has four top-level Role categories: Property, Trade, Supplier, and History Viewer.**

**Their sub-role titles define the person’s current participation and authority.**

**Pro indicates paid status in Property, Trade, and Supplier titles. Admin also requires paid access and therefore does not receive a separate Pro label.**

**For shared interaction or organization between people inside a Property, Business, or Facility, at least one party to the relationship must have a paid subscription.**

**A free account may create one Property and one Business.**

**Role and authority changes affect current and future access without rewriting previous contributions or historical attribution.**
