# ROUNDHOUSE — ROLES & PERMISSIONS

## Purpose

This document defines how people participate in Roundhouse and how authority and access are controlled.

Roundhouse separates three things:

**Role** — how someone is participating in an interaction.  
**Authority** — what that person is allowed to control.  
**Permission** — what that person can see and do.

Having a role does not automatically give someone access to everything within a Property, Business, or Job.

## 1. Base Roles

### Homeowner
The owner of the home and the primary authority over the Property.

### Home Team Member
Someone the Homeowner allows to participate in managing or caring for the home. Authority depends on the permissions given to them.

### Viewer
A person given authorized visibility into a Home or other applicable Entity without becoming a Team Member and without receiving contribution or management authority. Access is limited to what the Owner or authorized person grants.

A Home Team Member who is removed from the Home Team may remain connected as a Viewer where continued visibility is appropriate, without retaining the authority or access of a Home Team Member.

### Trade Professional
A person providing professional trade or service work. Access to one Property or Job does not automatically provide access to another.

A person may create their own Trade Business and become a Trade Professional. Being a Trade Professional by itself does not give that person authority to attach themselves to someone else's Property. They need appropriate access granted by an authorized participant in that Property.

### Trade Team Member
A person working as part of a Trade Business or team. Being a Trade Team Member does not automatically provide access to every client, Property, Job, invoice, or Business record.

There is **no Trade Viewer role**. Public or otherwise authorized viewing of a Trade Business or Trade Professional does not require someone to become a participant in that Trade Entity. A former Trade participant retains their own legitimate historical work and portfolio through their permanent Identity and historical attribution rather than being converted into a Trade Viewer.

### Commercial Management
A person responsible for managing a commercial Property or facility according to the authority they have been given.

### Commercial Team Member
Someone participating under Commercial Management. Access can be limited to particular Properties, areas, Jobs, records, or responsibilities.

### Supplier
A person or Business providing materials, products, or recurring goods or services. Access is limited to the information and functions necessary for their participation.

## 2. Active Roles and Historical Participation

An active role represents a person's current authority and access. Losing an active role does not erase the person's identity or the work they legitimately performed while holding that role.

A person who no longer has an active role may still see their own legitimate historical contributions through their permanent Identity and historical attribution. Historical visibility does not reopen access to the Property, Business, Job, client information, or other records that belonged to the former active role.

Viewer is an active, view-only Role where authorized visibility is intentionally granted. It is not a generic resting state for every former participant.

A historical record can continue to identify the role and Entity through which the person originally participated.

## 3. Pro and Authority Designations

**Pro is a membership designation, not a base role.** A person's underlying role, Pro status, and authority remain separate.

Authority designations may include **Owner, Co-Owner, Lead, and Manager** where applicable.

Examples include:

- **Home Pro (Owner)**
- **Home Pro (Co-Owner)**
- **Home Pro (Lead)**
- **Trade Pro (Owner)**
- **Trade Pro (Co-Owner)**
- **Trade Pro (Lead)**
- **Trade Pro (Manager)**
- **Trade Pro**
- **Commercial Pro**
- **Commercial Team Pro**

A Home Pro may add a **Home Team Member** without making that person a Home Pro. If that person is removed from the Home Team, their authority ends; where appropriate, the Homeowner may retain them as a Viewer with limited visibility.

A person whose Trade Pro status exists through a Trade Business loses that Business-derived Pro status when that participation ends unless another basis for active Trade Pro status exists. Their legitimate historical Trade work remains attributed to them without requiring a Trade Viewer role.

Ownership does not automatically make someone the operating Lead or Manager. Lead and Manager authority do not transfer ownership.

## 4. Manager Authority

**Manager is an authority designation, not a base Role.** An Owner or authorized person may delegate management of a Property, Job, Business workflow, or specific participants without transferring ownership.

A Manager may coordinate schedules, Daily Grinds, work, unfinished items, Resolutions, and participant workflow within their granted scope.

### Management Scope

Management may cover:

- **All Trade Professionals**
- **All Home Team Members**
- **Selected individuals from either group**

This includes active users and Temporary Profiles.

**One participant has one active Manager for the same scope of work.** Once management is delegated, the Owner does not simultaneously direct that participant. The Owner may **Take Back Management** of one, several, or all delegated participants at any time. A Manager cannot prevent or override this.

**Owner holds authority → Manager exercises delegated authority → Participant follows that Manager.**

## 5. Manage Team

Homeowners and authorized Trade Professionals have **Manage Team → Add Worker**.

**Name and phone are required; email is optional.** Adding someone creates a **Temporary Profile**, describing profile status rather than employment status.

The creator then assigns the participant to their **Business** or a **Property they have authority to manage**.

Temporary Profiles can receive schedules, Daily Grinds, tasks, workflow, and work history without activating Roundhouse.

## 6. Property Participants & Approval

Working at a Property does not itself authorize someone to add or manage participants. A Trade Professional needs sufficient Manager authority.

When Homeowner approval is required:

**Request → SMS to Homeowner → Open Roundhouse → Review participant, Role, requester and scope → Approve/Reject**

SMS does not constitute approval; final approval occurs and is recorded inside Roundhouse.

Before a Homeowner has joined or taken control of a Property, a legitimate Trade Professional managing it may create and manage necessary Temporary Profiles. Existing legitimate participants and history remain when the Homeowner later takes control.

## 7. Share & Activation

Either the **Homeowner** or authorized **Trade Professional** may use **Share** to ask a Temporary participant to complete their profile.

The participant remains associated with the **Property**. Share determines management after activation:

**Trade Professional sends Share → existing authorized management continues.**

**Homeowner sends Share → Homeowner manages after activation.**

The Trade Professional must then **Request Management** from the Homeowner to manage that participant again. The Homeowner may approve it and later **Take Back Management**.

## 8. Claiming a Temporary Profile

Creating or managing a Temporary Profile never gives someone ownership of that person's Identity.

A **Share invitation** connects the intended person to their Temporary Profile. Without Share, claiming an existing Temporary Profile requires verified matching of:

**Name + Phone + Email**

Once verified, the Temporary Profile connects to the person's permanent Roundhouse Identity without losing their existing **Role, Property participation, assignments, work, or history**.

**Temporary Profile → Verified Identity → Same Person → Same History**

## 9. Management Accountability

Authorized management activity may include:

**Assignments → contact/response → scheduling/rescheduling → missed work → completion → corrections/rework → follow-up → Resolutions**

Roundhouse preserves these events so Owners can see actual performance patterns—including delays, rework, missed appointments, management effort, and actual cost—without labeling participants good or bad.

**Manager authority determines who can manage. Exclusive management determines who gives direction. Timeline records what happened.**

## 10. Property Creation

Creating a Property does not establish ownership of that Property.

Homeowners, Home Team Members, Trade Professionals, Trade Team Members, Commercial Management, Commercial Team Members, and other authorized operational participants may create a Property when they have a legitimate reason to interact with that Property.

A Trade Team Member arriving at a new Job may create the Property so work can begin to be documented even though that person does not own the Property or the Trade Business.

Creation, ownership, authority, and access remain separate.

## 11. Permissions

Permissions determine what someone can **see and do**.

Permissions may be broad or limited to specific Properties, Businesses, Jobs, clients, areas of work, records, photos and communications, estimates and invoices, financial information, or functions within Roundhouse.

Access to one does not automatically provide access to the others.

## 12. Property Permissions

The Homeowner or someone given sufficient Property authority determines what participants can access within the Property, subject to delegated management authority.

A gardener may only need landscaping information. An electrician may only need electrical information. A designer might need access across several areas. The Homeowner can also choose broader access.

Being allowed to work at a Property does not automatically mean being allowed to see everything associated with that Property or manage other participants.

## 13. Business Permissions

A Business Owner or authorized Lead determines what people working through that Business can access.

A worker can be assigned to a particular Job and receive the information necessary to perform that Job without gaining access to every client or Job belonging to the Business.

Business membership does not automatically provide access to the entire Business.

## 14. Client Information

Assignment to a Job does not automatically provide unrestricted access to the Business's client information.

A worker can receive the information necessary to perform the Job—such as the client's name, Job location, scope of work, schedule, and necessary Property information—without automatically receiving private contact information or the Business's complete history with that client.

## 15. Financial Permissions

Estimates, invoices, billing, payments, and financial reporting have their own permissions.

Financial authority can be limited by both **function and scope**.

A worker might be allowed to create or complete an invoice for the Job they are currently performing without being able to see other customer invoices, previous billing for that Property, Business revenue, financial reports, other workers' billing, or the Business's complete customer history.

A Business Owner may give a bookkeeper or another authorized person broader financial access.

Permission to perform work does not automatically provide permission to see the Business's finances.

## 16. Layered Permissions

Permissions can exist at different levels.

A Business can determine which of its people may participate in a Job. The Property can separately determine what that Business and its participants may access within the Property. Delegated Manager authority further determines who may direct specific participants.

A person receives access appropriate to the particular interaction rather than automatically inheriting everything available to the Business, Property, or other participants.

## 17. Temporary and Job-Specific Authority

Authority and permissions can be temporary and scoped to a particular Job, Property, function, or participant.

For example, a Trade Team Member could receive permission to prepare an invoice for the Job they are completing without receiving permanent billing authority for the Business.

When that interaction ends, the temporary permission can end without changing the person's permanent identity or historical participation.

## 18. Historical Access

Changing someone's current permissions, management authority, or active role does not erase Roundhouse records that already exist.

A person may lose access to a Property, Business, Job, client, management relationship, or financial function while the historical record of what occurred remains preserved.

A former participant can see their own legitimate historical contributions without regaining former authority or access.

Current access and historical record are separate.

## Governing Rule

**Role identifies how someone is participating. Authority determines what they can control. Permissions determine what they can see and do. Management may be delegated without transferring ownership, and one participant has one active Manager for the same scope. Active roles and authority can end without erasing historical participation. Viewer is an explicitly view-only Role; no Trade Viewer role is required.**

Access should be sufficient for the interaction without automatically exposing everything belonging to the Property, Business, client, or other participants.
