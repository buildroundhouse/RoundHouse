# ROUNDHOUSE — ENTITY MODEL

## Purpose

An Entity is a Property, Business, or History that exists in RoundHouse.

A person connects to an Entity and has a Role within that Entity.

The Entity is where that person’s relevant Timeline, records, permissions, communication, and activity are shown.

**No communication, documentation, or operational activity happens outside of an Entity.**

RoundHouse has three types of Entities:

- **Property**
- **Business**
- **History**

## Property

Property types:

- Residential Property
- Commercial Property / Facility

## Business

Business types:

- Trade Professional Business
- Supplier Business

## History

There is one type of History Entity.

History is private to one person.

Viewer is the only Role available inside History.

History shows that person’s combined legitimate Timeline across the Properties and Businesses they have participated in.

History is limited-function. It allows the Viewer to use **Share RoundHouse** and to search for, connect to, or begin setting up a Property or Business.

All other communication, documentation, and operational activity happens inside a Property or Business Entity.

## Roles and Entities

A Role exists within an Entity.

A Role does not exist independently of an Entity.
